Network Discovery
?
Scans the local network for vulnerable TVs — including every device family in the CVE database (Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Android TV, Roku, Vizio, Hisense, TCL, Chromecast) via their signature ports. Each result shows open ports, the matched CVEs (colored by severity), and status. Use "Check" to verify a device before exploitation, "Exploit" to stage a payload, or "Patch" to execute that family's remediation commands and fix the matched CVEs on the device.
ℹ️ Scan the local network to discover vulnerable TVs and IoT devices.
Exploit Payload Generator
?
Generates family-specific exploit payloads that connect back to this C2 server. Select target IP and device family, then click Generate to create the payload.
⚠️ For authorized security testing only. Use only on devices you own or have permission to test.
CVE Monitor & Patch Management
?
Fetches new smart TV CVEs from NVD (runs automatically at every C2 start and on "Update CVEs"). New entries land in the CVE Feed on the right and in CVE_UPDATES.log at the repo root. Click any CVE for its NVD advisory or whitepaper. Use "Patch" on a scan result or device card to execute that device family's remediation commands against the matched TVs. With an AI key configured, a working exploit module is auto-generated per new CVE.
ℹ️ Monitor known CVEs affecting smart TVs and apply patches to vulnerable devices found on the network.
AI Provider Keys (Auto-Exploit Generation)
?
Keys used to generate a working exploit module for each new CVE automatically. Providers: OpenRouter (Uncensored), Venice AI (Uncensored V 1.2 / Qwen 3.6 Plus Uncensored / GLM 4.7 Flash Heretic), and Abliteration.ai (Abliterated Large v2). Keys can also be supplied via .env (checked first by run_c2.bat) or c2_data/ai_keys.json. Keys are masked after saving.
⚠️ Keys are stored locally in c2_data/ai_keys.json. Never commit this file.
